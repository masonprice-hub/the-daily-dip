MADE FRESH, MADE WITH CARE

How to make
caramel apple magic

A cozy kitchen classic with crisp apples, smooth caramel, and room for all your favorite toppings.

MAKES6 apples YOU’LL NEEDA little patience & a grown-up helper

Hot caramel can burn. Ask an adult to help with melting and dipping, and follow the caramel package directions.

GATHER YOUR INGREDIENTS

The good stuff

  • 6 medium apples
  • 1 package soft caramels
  • 2 tbsp water (or as directed on the caramel package)
  • 6 wooden sticks
  • Optional chopped nuts, sprinkles, or mini chocolate chips

You’ll also need a saucepan or microwave-safe bowl, a spoon, a baking tray, and parchment paper.

LET’S MAKE SOMETHING SWEET

One little step at a time

  1. Wash the apples and dry them completely.
  2. Carefully remove the stems and push a wooden stick into the top of each apple.
  3. Line a baking tray with parchment paper.
  4. With an adult’s help, melt the caramels with the water according to the package directions. Stir until smooth.
  5. Dip each apple in the caramel and turn it until coated.
  6. Add your favorite toppings while the caramel is still soft.
  7. Set the apples on the prepared tray and let the caramel firm up before serving.
  8. Share, enjoy, and follow safe food-handling and storage instructions.

A LITTLE SOMETHING EXTRA

This week’s special

Cinnamon Toast Crunch, creamy white chocolate, and a buttery caramel finish. It’s a crunchy, sweet twist on our favorite caramel apple.

CINNAMON TOAST CRUNCH • WHITE CHOCOLATE • CARAMEL

Cinnamon Crunch
caramel apples

Makes4 apples PrepAbout 30 minutes, plus setting time

Gather your ingredients

  • 4 crisp apples, washed and completely dried
  • 1 package soft caramels, plus water as directed on package
  • 1 cup white chocolate chips or melting wafers
  • 2 cups Cinnamon Toast Crunch cereal, lightly crushed
  • 4 wooden apple sticks
  • Optional extra caramel for drizzling

You’ll also need parchment paper and a baking tray. Check ingredient labels for allergens before serving.

YOUR NEW FAVORITE FALL TREAT

Make the
weekly special

A few simple steps turn a fresh apple into a crunchy, chocolatey, caramel-dipped treat.

Ask an adult to help with hot caramel and melted chocolate. Follow package directions and take care to avoid burns.

  1. Line a baking tray with parchment. Wash the apples, dry them completely, and carefully press a wooden stick into each stem end.
  2. Put the Cinnamon Toast Crunch in a sealed bag and gently crush it into small, crunchy pieces. Pour the crumbs into a shallow bowl.
  3. With an adult’s help, melt the caramels with water according to the package directions. Stir until smooth.
  4. Dip each apple in the caramel, turning it to coat. Let the extra caramel drip off, then set the apple on the lined tray until the caramel is just tacky.
  5. Melt the white chocolate according to its package directions. Drizzle it over the apples or dip the caramel-coated apples in it.
  6. While the chocolate is still soft, sprinkle the crushed cereal over each apple and gently press it into the coating. Drizzle with extra caramel if you like.
  7. Place the apples on the tray and let the coating set before serving. Store and handle them according to the package directions and safe food-handling guidance.
